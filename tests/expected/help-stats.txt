usage: lc stats [--manifest] [--json] [--color=WHEN]

Show how many problems you have solved and attempted: in all, by
difficulty and by tag. A problem is solved once a submission of it was
accepted, and attempted when it was submitted but never accepted. Only
'lc submit' counts; your progress is kept in progress.tsv in
$XDG_DATA_HOME/lcclone, and a copy of each submission in submissions/
next to it.

With --manifest, show instead how much of the problem set listed in
the problem directory's MANIFEST.tsv has been written (for authors).

Options:
  --manifest         report on MANIFEST.tsv
  --json             print JSON
  --color=WHEN       colour the output: auto (the default), always or
                     never
  -h, --help         show this help
--- stderr
--- exit 0
