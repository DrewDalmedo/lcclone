# Capitalize the Title.

@example "ON" has two letters, so it becomes "on"; the other words have three or more.
"dOGS ON tHe mOON"
=> "Dogs on The Moon"

@example A one-letter first word stays lowercase, like every short word.
"A TALE oF tWO CITIES"
=> "a Tale of Two Cities"

@example A single word of two letters.
"Up"
=> "up"

# A single letter, uppercase in the input.
"Q"
=> "q"

# Two letters next to three: the edge of the rule.
"xY XyZ"
=> "xy Xyz"

# The last word has one, two or three letters.
"QUICK BROWN m"
=> "Quick Brown m"

"sweet AS"
=> "Sweet as"

"far AND aWaY bUs"
=> "Far And Away Bus"

# Already in the target form: nothing changes.
"Owl in a Box"
=> "Owl in a Box"

# Long words in alternating case: the letters after the first are lowered.
"sTrAnGe WeAtHeR wArNiNgS"
=> "Strange Weather Warnings"
