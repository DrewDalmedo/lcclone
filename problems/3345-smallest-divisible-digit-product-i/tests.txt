# Smallest Divisible Digit Product I.

@example 23 gives 2 * 3 = 6, not a multiple of 4. The next number, 24, gives 8, which is.
23
4
=> 24

@example 88 and 89 give 64 and 72, neither a multiple of 7. 90 has a 0 digit, so its product is 0.
88
7
=> 90

@example 3 * 6 = 18 is already a multiple of 6, so n itself is the answer.
36
6
=> 36

# The smallest inputs: t = 1 accepts every number.
1
1
=> 1

# The largest n: 100 has 0 digits.
100
7
=> 100

# A one-digit answer is its own product.
3
7
=> 7

# No single digit is a multiple of 10, so the search reaches 10.
1
10
=> 10

# n has a 0 digit, though its other digit alone would not do.
30
7
=> 30

20
3
=> 20

# 10 divides 5 * 2 without a 0 digit.
51
10
=> 52

# No number in the thirties pairs a 5 with an even digit, so 40 it is.
31
10
=> 40

# Neither digit is a multiple of t, but their product is.
61
4
=> 62

42
8
=> 42

# 7 is prime: only a digit 7 (or a 0) makes a multiple of it.
11
7
=> 17

# Nothing from 91 to 99 works, so the answer has three digits.
91
10
=> 100
