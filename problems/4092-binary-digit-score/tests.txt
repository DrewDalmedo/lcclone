# Binary Digit Score.

@example 29 is 11101 (four 1s) and 36 is 100100 (two). 4 >= 2, so the score is 4 * 2 = 8.
29
36
=> 8

@example 18 is 10010 (two 1s) and 56 is 111000 (three). 2 < 3, so the score is 3 / 2 rounded down, 1.
18
56
=> 1

@example 21 is 10101 and 11 is 1011, three 1s each. A tie uses the product, 3 * 3 = 9.
21
11
=> 9
