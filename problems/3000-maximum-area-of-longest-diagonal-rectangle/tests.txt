# Maximum Area of Longest Diagonal Rectangle. The comments give squared
# diagonals, l^2 + w^2, which order the diagonals exactly.

@example The squared diagonals are 40, 34 and 32, so [6,2] has the longest diagonal, though [4,4] has the larger area.
[[6,2],[3,5],[4,4]]
=> 12

@example [7,1] and [5,5] share the longest diagonal (49 + 1 = 25 + 25 = 50), and [5,5] has the larger area.
[[7,1],[3,6],[5,5],[2,4]]
=> 25

@example A single screen is always the answer.
[[12,5]]
=> 60

# The smallest screen there can be.
[[1,1]]
=> 1

# Sides at the upper bound: a squared diagonal of 20000 and an area of
# 10000, too much for a byte. [100,100] beats the screens just below it.
[[99,100],[100,100],[100,99],[1,100]]
=> 10000

# A tie at 65 (1 + 64 = 16 + 49) with the larger area second: keeping the
# first screen of a tie is wrong.
[[1,8],[4,7]]
=> 28

# The same tie with the larger area first: keeping the last screen of a tie
# is wrong.
[[7,4],[8,1]]
=> 28

# Three ways to make 325 (1 + 324, 36 + 289, 100 + 225), the largest area
# in the middle.
[[6,17],[10,15],[1,18],[2,6]]
=> 150

# Six screens tie at 9425; the largest area, 64 * 73, comes first.
[[64,73],[4,97],[20,95],[31,92],[41,88],[55,80]]
=> 4672

# The largest area doesn't have the longest diagonal (98 against 101).
[[7,7],[1,10]]
=> 10

# 5 + 5 is more than 1 + 8, but 50 is less than 65: adding the sides
# instead of their squares is wrong.
[[5,5],[1,8]]
=> 8

# The longest side alone doesn't decide: 8 is more than 6, but 72 is more
# than 65.
[[1,8],[6,6]]
=> 36

# A larger area with a shorter diagonal comes after the longest one (82
# against 72).
[[1,9],[6,6]]
=> 9

# A diagonal of exactly 13 against one just over 13 (169 against 170):
# rounding the square root down to a whole number makes them tie.
[[5,12],[1,13]]
=> 13

# Near the top two diagonals differ by about 0.007 (19604 against 19602):
# comparing square roots with a tolerance of 0.01 makes them tie.
[[98,100],[99,99]]
=> 9800

# Repeats and swapped sides are screens of the same size.
[[3,8],[8,3],[3,8],[2,8]]
=> 24
