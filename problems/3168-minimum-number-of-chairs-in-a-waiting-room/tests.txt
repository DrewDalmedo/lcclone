# Minimum Number of Chairs in a Waiting Room.

@example Five people walk in, but the first two have left before the next three arrive, so 3 chairs are enough.
"EELLEEEL"
=> 3

@example Two people are inside together after the second event and again after the eighth, and never more than two.
"EELLELEEL"
=> 2

@example All three are inside together after the third event.
"EEELLL"
=> 3
