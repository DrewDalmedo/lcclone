# Count Distinct Numbers on Board.

@example House 8 tells 7; house 7 tells 2, 3 and 6; house 6 tells 5; house 5 tells 4. Houses 2 to 8 know the news. House 1 never does, since every x mod 1 is 0.
8
=> 7

@example House 1 knows the news from the start, and there is no other house.
1
=> 1

@example 2 mod 1 and 2 mod 2 are both 0, so house 2 tells nobody.

=> 1
