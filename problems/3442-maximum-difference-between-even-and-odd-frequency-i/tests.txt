# Maximum Difference Between Even and Odd Frequency I. A letter's tally
# is how often it occurs in s; letters that don't occur have none.

@example Tallies: a 3, n 2, b 1 and s 1. The best choice is a with n, giving 3 minus 2.
"bananas"
=> 1

@example The only odd tally is m's 1, and the smallest even one is p's 2. Letters missing from s don't give an even tally of 0.
"mississippi"
=> -1

@example Tallies r 5, g 4, b 2, t 1: the largest odd one minus the smallest even one.
"grbrtgrbgrgr"
=> 3

# The shortest string: one letter twice, one once.
"cdc"
=> -1

# One odd tally (3) and one even tally (2).
"wqwqq"
=> 1

# All 26 letters occur: q 5 times, m twice, the rest once.
"abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyzmqqqq"
=> 3

# Odd tallies 1 and 3, even tallies 4 and 6: the smallest even one counts.
"dcbdadcdbcdbcd"
=> -1

# The most common letter, k (8 times), has an even tally and is no use.
"jkjkjkjkjkjkjkhkh"
=> 5

# Odd tallies 1, 5, 9 and even tallies 2, 4, 8.
"bebebebebdadcfcfcfcfcfcfcfcfc"
=> 7

# Seven letters once and z six times.
"pzqzrzsztzuzv"
=> -5

# Odd tally 3 against even tallies 10 and 12.
"aaabbbbbbbbbbcccccccccccc"
=> -7

# The largest answer: an odd tally of 97 and an even tally of 2.
"eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeffg"
=> 95

# The smallest answer: odd tallies of 1 against an even tally of 98.
"ihhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhj"
=> -97
