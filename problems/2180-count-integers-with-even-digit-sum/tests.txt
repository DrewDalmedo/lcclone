# Count Integers With Even Digit Sum.

@example The blue doors are on houses 2, 4, 6, 8, 11 and 13.
13
=> 6

@example Six blue doors up to house 13 (as in Example 1), then houses 15, 17, 19 and 20; house 21 adds up to 3.
21
=> 10

@example 49 of the houses 1 to 99 get a blue door; house 100 adds up to 1, so it doesn't.
100
=> 49

# The smallest street: house 1 has an odd total.
1
=> 0

# The last house counts when its total is even.
2
=> 1

# Single digits: just the even numbers.
9
=> 4

# 10 adds up to 1, so the count stays at 4 (10 / 2 would say 5).
10
=> 4

# 11 adds up to 2.
11
=> 5

# Odd num, even total (1 + 9 = 10).
19
=> 9

# Just past the first hundred: 101 adds up to 2.
101
=> 50

# A multiple of 10 with an odd total (2 + 1 + 0 = 3).
210
=> 104

# 500 adds up to 5.
500
=> 249

# The largest answer, reached at 998, 999 and 1000; 1000 is the only
# four-digit house number, with total 1.
998
=> 499

999
=> 499

1000
=> 499
