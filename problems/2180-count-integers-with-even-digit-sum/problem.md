The houses on a street carry the numbers `1`, `2`, `3`, ... up to `num`,
one number per house, so the last house is `num` itself. A house gets a
blue door when the decimal digits of its number add up to an even total:
house `352` gets one (`3 + 5 + 2 = 10`), house `47` doesn't
(`4 + 7 = 11`).

Return how many houses on the street get a blue door.

@examples

## Constraints

- `1 <= num <= 1000`
