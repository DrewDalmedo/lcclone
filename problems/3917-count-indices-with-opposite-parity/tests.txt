# Count Indices With Opposite Parity. The entries of each answer add up to
# (number of even values) * (number of odd values).

@example 3 is odd and has three even values after it (8, 2, 2); 8 has one odd value after it (5); 5 has two even values after it; the two 2s have no odd value after them.
[3,8,5,2,2]
=> [3,1,2,0,0]

@example 14 is even and is followed by the odd values 9, 9 and 11; each 9 and the 6 have one mismatched value after them.
[14,9,9,6,11]
=> [3,1,1,1,0]

@example All values are even, so no two are mismatched.
[10,4,6]
=> [0,0,0]
