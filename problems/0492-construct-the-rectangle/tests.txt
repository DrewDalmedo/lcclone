# Construct the Rectangle. Results are [L, W], the longer side first.

@example 18 is 18 * 1, 9 * 2 or 6 * 3, and 6 and 3 differ the least.
18
=> [6,3]

@example 29 is prime, so the only rectangle is a strip of 29 by 1.
29
=> [29,1]

@example 225 = 15 * 15 is a square: its sides differ by 0.
225
=> [15,15]

# The smallest area, a single square.
1
=> [1,1]

# The smallest area with no square: a strip.
2
=> [2,1]

# 3 * 4: the floor of the root, 3, divides 12, but the longer side comes
# first, so [3,4] is wrong.
12
=> [4,3]

# 3000 * 3001, the same shape at full size.
9003000
=> [3001,3000]

# The largest prime in range: the search must go all the way down to
# W = 1, and L does not fit in 16 bits.
9999991
=> [9999991,1]

# 3162 * 3162, the largest square in range; W does not fit in 8 bits.
9998244
=> [3162,3162]

# The top of the range, 2^7 * 5^7.
10000000
=> [3200,3125]

# Two primes close to the root of their product.
9922331
=> [3163,3137]

9734399
=> [3121,3119]

# A small prime times a large one.
9999993
=> [3333331,3]

# Many divisors: 720720 = 2^4 * 3^2 * 5 * 7 * 11 * 13.

=> [858,840]

# 2^23 is no square, so the sides are 2^12 and 2^11.
8388608
=> [4096,2048]
