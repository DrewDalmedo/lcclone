# Maximize Expression of Three Elements.

@example Take a = 8 and b = 6, and subtract c = -4: 8 + 6 + 4 = 18.
[3,-4,8,6,1]
=> 18

@example The two 7s sit at different positions, so both can be added; subtracting 2 gives 12.
[7,2,7,5]
=> 12

@example With three entries all of them are used, and subtracting -9 is best: -3 - 6 + 9 = 0.
[-9,-3,-6]
=> 0
