# Arranging Coins.

@example Rows 1 to 4 take 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10 coins. The 2 coins left start row 5, which needs 5.
12
=> 4

@example 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15, so the coins fill exactly five rows.
15
=> 5

@example With one coin fewer than 15, row 5 ends up one coin short.
14
=> 4

# The smallest n: one coin fills row 1.
1
=> 1
