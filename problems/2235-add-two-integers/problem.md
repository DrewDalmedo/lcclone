difficulty: easy

---
You are handed two whole numbers, `num1` and `num2`, each somewhere between
`-100` and `100`. Return the value you get by adding them together.

Either number may be negative or zero, so the total can be negative or zero
as well. The ordinary `+` operator is allowed here.

@examples

## Constraints

- `-100 <= num1, num2 <= 100`
