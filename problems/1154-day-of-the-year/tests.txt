# Day of the Year.

@example 2004 is a leap year, so January's 31 days and February's 29 come first: 31 + 29 + 15 = 75.
"2004-03-15"
=> 75

@example 1900 is a multiple of 100 but not of 400, so its February has 28 days: 31 + 28 + 1 = 60.
"1900-03-01"
=> 60

@example Six months of 31, 28, 31, 30, 31 and 30 days come first, 181 in all, and 181 + 4 = 185.
"1987-07-04"
=> 185

# The first and last dates allowed.
"1900-01-01"
=> 1

"2019-12-31"
=> 365

# 1900 is not a leap year: its last day is day 365.
"1900-12-31"
=> 365

# 2000 is a leap year (a multiple of 400): the leap day, the day after it,
# and day 366.
"2000-02-29"
=> 60

"2000-03-01"
=> 61

"2000-12-31"
=> 366

# The leap day of an ordinary leap year.
"1996-02-29"
=> 60

# Before March a leap year counts like any other: February 1 is day 32.
"1904-02-01"
=> 32

# December 1 in a common year and in a leap year.
"1997-12-01"
=> 335

"2008-12-01"
=> 336

# Months 08 and 09 and days 08 and 09, which an octal parse of "08" and
# "09" gets wrong.
"1989-08-09"
=> 221

"1960-09-08"
=> 252

# October and November: a month-length table with one 30-day month in the
# wrong place (October for November, or September and October swapped)
# only shows up in the months that follow the mistake.
"1943-10-31"
=> 304

"2012-11-30"
=> 335
