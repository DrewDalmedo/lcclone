# Harshad Number. -1 means x is not a Harshad number.

@example 4 + 2 = 6, and 42 = 7 * 6.
42
=> 6

@example 3 + 5 = 8, and 35 leaves 3 over when divided by 8.
35
=> -1

@example 1 + 0 + 0 = 1, and every number is a multiple of 1.
100
=> 1

# The smallest x.
1
=> 1

# A single digit is its own total.
9
=> 9

# The total of 10 is 1.
10
=> 1

# A multiple of 10: the total is its tens digit.
70
=> 7

# 99 has the largest total, 18, and is not a multiple of it.
99
=> -1

# Two equal digits never work: x is 11 * d, which 2 * d doesn't divide.
11
=> -1

66
=> -1

# Totals above 9 that divide x.
48
=> 12

84
=> 12

81
=> 9

# Totals that don't divide x.
19
=> -1

98
=> -1
