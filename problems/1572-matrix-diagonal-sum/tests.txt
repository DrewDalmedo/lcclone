# Matrix Diagonal Sum.

@example The four corners give 6 + 11 + 13 + 1 = 31, and the middle cell, on both lines, adds its 10 just once.
[[6,2,11],[3,10,4],[13,5,1]]
=> 41

@example With an even side the lines share no cell, so all eight numbers count: 11 on one line and 30 on the other.
[[3,10,20,6],[11,2,8,15],[14,9,1,12],[7,13,16,5]]
=> 41

@example Every cell of a 2 x 2 grid lies on one of the lines.
[[2,9],[4,6]]
=> 21

# A single cell, counted once.
[[37]]
=> 37

# The smallest values.
[[1,1],[1,1]]
=> 4

# The largest values, odd side: five cells.
[[100,100,100],[100,100,100],[100,100,100]]
=> 500

# Large neighbours around small diagonal cells: adding a wrong cell shows.
[[1,50,2],[60,3,70],[4,80,5]]
=> 15

# Odd side with a large middle cell: counting it twice gives 348.
[[12,40,41,42,13],[43,22,44,23,45],[46,47,100,48,49],[50,24,51,25,52],[14,53,54,55,15]]
=> 248

# Even side: nothing is shared, so nothing is taken off (mat[3][3] is 2).
[[9,1,1,1,1,8],[1,7,1,1,6,1],[1,1,5,4,1,1],[1,1,3,2,1,1],[1,10,1,1,11,1],[12,1,1,1,1,13]]
=> 90
