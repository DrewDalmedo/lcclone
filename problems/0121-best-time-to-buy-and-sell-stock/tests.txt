# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock.

@example Buy on day 0 at 3 and sell on day 1 at 8. Buying at 1 on day 4 comes too late to beat that.
[3,8,2,6,1,4]
=> 5

@example The price only falls, so the best choice is not to trade.
[9,7,4,1]
=> 0

@example With a single day there is nothing to sell on.
[5]
=> 0

# Flat prices.
[2,2,2]
=> 0

# The lowest price comes after the highest one.
[10000,0]
=> 0

# Extremes.
[0,10000]
=> 10000

[1,10000]
=> 9999

# The best purchase is not the first day, and the best sale not the last.
[4,1,7,3,9,2]
=> 8

# A new low followed by a higher high.
[0,5,0,6]
=> 6

[6,1,3,2,4,7]
=> 6

# The minimum is the last day.
[5,6,7,8,1]
=> 3

# Random small cases from gen.c (lc check --regen makes them).
@gen 1 small

=> 15

[20,7,8,14,9]

@gen 3 small
[10,5,20,7,19,11,0,1]
