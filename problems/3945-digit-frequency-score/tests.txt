# Digit Frequency Score.

@example 4 occurs three times and scores 4 * 3 = 12; 8 occurs once and scores 8.
4448
=> 20

@example The two 3s score 6 between them, and the three 0s score nothing.
30300
=> 6

@example A single digit scores its own value.
7
=> 7

# The smallest n.
1
=> 1

# The largest n, ten digits: a single 1 and nine 0s.
1000000000
=> 1

# The largest score: nine 9s.
999999999
=> 81

# One digit repeated: counting each distinct digit once would give 2.
222222222
=> 18

# One 2 and three 3s: 2 + 3 * 3. Squaring the counts, or counting only how
# often digits occur, gives other totals.
2333
=> 11

# Every digit from 1 to 9 once.
123456789
=> 45

# Non-zero digits only at the two ends.
900000009
=> 18

100000001
=> 2

# Zeros between the other digits.
908070605
=> 35

# Two digits taking turns: five 9s and four 8s.
989898989
=> 77

# A trailing zero.
10
=> 1
