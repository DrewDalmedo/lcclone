# Number of Valid Clock Times.

@example The hour can be 02, 12 or 22, and the minute any of 40 to 49: 3 * 10 times.
"?2:4?"
=> 30

@example The hour is one of 10 to 19, and the minute ends in 5 with a first digit from 0 to 5: 10 * 6 times.
"1?:?5"
=> 60

@example An hour ending in 8 can only be 08 or 18 (28 is too late), and the minute is free: 2 * 60 times.
"?8:??"
=> 120

# No '?' at all: the one time itself.
"21:37"
=> 1

# Every digit hidden: all 1440 times of the day.
"??:??"
=> 1440

# Hidden first hour digit before a 3: 03, 13 and 23.
"?3:15"
=> 3

# Before a 4 only 04 and 14, since 24 is no hour.
"?4:15"
=> 2

# The two ends of the range, 0 and 9.
"?0:52"
=> 3

"?9:06"
=> 2

# Hidden second hour digit: after a 2 only 20 to 23, after a 0 or 1 all ten.
"2?:30"
=> 4

"0?:30"
=> 10

"1?:00"
=> 10

# Both hour digits hidden: 24 hours, not 3 * 10.
"??:47"
=> 24

# Minute digits alone: tens digit hidden (0 to 5), units digit hidden, both.
"05:?9"
=> 6

"05:5?"
=> 10

"16:??"
=> 60

# Both halves partly hidden, so the counts multiply.
"2?:?0"
=> 24

"??:?3"
=> 144

"?4:3?"
=> 20

"0?:??"
=> 600
