# Smallest Even Multiple.

@example 9 is odd, so the list goes on to 18, which is even.
9
=> 18

@example 14 is already even, and it is the first entry of its own list.
14
=> 14

@example The list for 1 is 1, 2, 3, ...; the first even entry is 2.
1
=> 2

# The smallest even n is its own answer, not 4.
2
=> 2

# The largest n, which is even.
150
=> 150

# The largest odd n gives the largest result.
149
=> 298
