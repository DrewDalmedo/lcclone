title: Count Odd Numbers in an Interval Range
difficulty: easy
tags: math
signature: int countOdds(int low, int high)
---
Look at the whole numbers from `low` up to `high`, with both `low` and
`high` themselves among them. Return how many of them are odd, that is,
leave a remainder of `1` when divided by `2`. Zero is even.

@examples

## Constraints

- `0 <= low <= high <= 10^9`

## Follow-up

The range can hold a billion numbers. Can you find the count without
looking at them one by one?
