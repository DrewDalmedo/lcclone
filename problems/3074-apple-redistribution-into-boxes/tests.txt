# Apple Redistribution into Boxes.

@example There are 12 apples. No single box holds them all, but the boxes of 7 and 5 hold exactly 12.
[4,6,2]
[3,5,7,1]
=> 2

@example The bag of 9 is bigger than every box, so its apples are spread over the boxes of 4, 3 and 2, which hold exactly 9.
[9]
[2,3,2,4]
=> 3

@example The box of 10 takes all 9 apples.
[3,3,3]
[10,1]
=> 1

# The smallest input.
[1]
[1]
=> 1

# A single box, filled exactly.
[20,30]
[50]
=> 1

# The biggest box comes last.
[5,5]
[1,1,1,1,10]
=> 1

# The two largest boxes hold exactly the 20 apples.
[10,10]
[5,15,3,2]
=> 2

# One bag is bigger than every box; five boxes of 10 hold it exactly.
[50]
[10,10,10,10,10,7]
=> 5

# More boxes than bags: the largest box alone is one apple short.
[7]
[1,2,3,4,5,6]
=> 2

# All boxes the same size: 15 apples need four boxes of 4.
[7,8]
[4,4,4,4,4]
=> 4

# Every box is needed, with an exact fit and without one.
[3,4]
[1,2,4]
=> 3

[2,2,3]
=> 3
