# Minimum Deletions for At Most K Distinct Characters.

@example Put in the stamps for a (5 occurrences) and for b or r (2 each); the other 4 characters are skipped.
"abracadabra"
2
=> 4

@example Only l, e and v occur, so all three stamps fit.
"level"
3
=> 0

@example With one stamp, i or s (4 times each) is the best choice, and the other 7 characters are skipped.
"mississippi"
1
=> 7

# A single character and the smallest k.
"q"
1
=> 0

# k is larger than the whole string.
"q"
16
=> 0

# The answer counts characters, not letters.
"aaabbbccc"
1
=> 6

# The rarest letters come last, in the alphabet and in s.
"aaaabbbbcccyz"
3
=> 2

# The rarest letters come first.
"abzzzzyyyy"
2
=> 2

# A tie at the cut-off between the kept letters and the skipped ones.
"aabbccd"
2
=> 3

# One stamp, two letters tied for the most.
"xxyy"
1
=> 2

# 16 different letters: one stamp gives the largest possible answer.
"zyxwvutsrqponmlk"
1
=> 15

"zyxwvutsrqponmlk"
15
=> 1

# Exactly k different letters.
"zyxwvutsrqponmlk"
16
=> 0

# 16 copies of one letter.
"kkkkkkkkkkkkkkkk"
1
=> 0

# One frequent letter and many single ones.
"ttttttttabcdefgh"
2
=> 7
