# Number Of Rectangles That Can Form The Largest Square.

@example Sheet by sheet, the largest tiles have sides 9, 6, 9 and 2. Nothing beats 9, and the first and third sheets reach it.
[[9,13],[14,6],[10,9],[11,2]]
=> 2

@example Three sheets have a shorter side of 6. [1,20] is by far the longest sheet, yet its largest tile is only 1 across.
[[10,6],[6,8],[11,6],[1,20]]
=> 3

@example [15,2] has the longest side, but its largest tile has side 2; only [3,4] yields a tile of side 3.
[[15,2],[3,4]]
=> 1

# A single sheet.
[[1,2]]
=> 1

# [2,5] has a side of 5, the largest tile side, but its other side is
# shorter, so it can't yield a tile of side 5.
[[5,9],[2,5],[5,7]]
=> 2

# Two entries with the same lengths are two sheets.
[[4,7],[4,7],[2,9]]
=> 2

# Every sheet reaches the largest side, with the shorter side first or second.
[[4,9],[9,4],[4,6]]
=> 3

# Ties at a smaller side come first; the largest side turns up only at the end, so
# the count must start again.
[[3,8],[8,3],[3,5],[7,4]]
=> 1

# Ties at the largest side at the start, in the middle and at the end.
[[6,7],[2,3],[9,6],[1,4],[6,100]]
=> 3

# The first number of the first sheet is not its shorter side.
[[8,2],[3,5],[5,3]]
=> 2

# Sides at the bounds. Their areas would overflow a 32-bit int.
