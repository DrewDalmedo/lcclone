# Number of Segments in a String.

@example The segments are "red-eye", "flight", "#42", "leaves", "at" and "6:15"; the spaces around them are only separators.
"  red-eye flight #42 leaves at 6:15  "
=> 6

@example "..." holds no space, so it is a segment like the two words beside it.
"wait ... what!"
=> 3

@example Spaces alone make no segment.
"    "
=> 0

# The empty string.
""
=> 0

# A single space and a single character.
" "
=> 0

"x"
=> 1

# The most spaces: 300 of them and nothing else.
"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            "
=> 0

# The longest segment: 300 characters with no space among them.
"Zq9!@#$%^&*()_+-=',.:abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZZq9!@#$%^&*()_+-=',.:abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZZq9!@#$%^&*()_+-=',.:abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZZq9!@#$%^&*()_+-=',.:abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123"
=> 1

# The most segments: 150 one-character segments, each after a single space.
" k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' . k 7 - Q ' ."
=> 150

# Spaces only before, or only after, the one segment.
"   lead"
=> 1

"trail   "
=> 1

# A run of spaces is one separator: counting spaces and adding 1 gives 8.
"one   two    three"
=> 3

# Digits and lone punctuation marks are segments too.
"go - 42 ' ok"
=> 5

# Punctuation does not split a segment.
"x,y"
=> 1

# Spaces at both ends and between two segments.
"  @home  ,  "
=> 2

# Random small cases from gen.c (lc check --regen makes them).
@gen 1 small
"--,,-1  "
=> 1

@gen 2 small
"   ,  "
=> 1

@gen 3 small
"a   1-,1a,"
=> 2

@gen 4 small
","
=> 1

@gen 5 small
"1 "
=> 1

@gen 6 small
"'1"
=> 1

@gen 7 small
"-a1,  ',"
=> 2
