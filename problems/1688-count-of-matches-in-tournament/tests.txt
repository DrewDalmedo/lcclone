# Count of Matches in Tournament.

@example Six teams play 3 matches; of the 3 left, two play and one gets a bye; the last 2 play the final: 3 + 1 + 1.
6
=> 5

@example 11 teams: 5 matches and a bye leave 6, then 3 matches leave 3, then 1 match and a bye leave 2, then the final: 5 + 3 + 1 + 1.
11
=> 10

@example A lone team is the champion without playing.
1
=> 0
