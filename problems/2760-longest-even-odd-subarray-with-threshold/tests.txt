# Longest Even Odd Subarray With Threshold. 0 means no chain at all.

@example 4, 9, 6, 1, 8 switches parity at every step and stays at or under 9; the 8 after it repeats a parity.
[7,4,9,6,1,8,8,3]
9
=> 5

@example 30 is above the threshold and cuts the chain after 12, 5; the 7 after it is odd, so nothing starts there.
[12,5,30,7]
20
=> 2

@example Every element is odd, so no chain can begin.
[5,11,3]
50
=> 0

# A single even element within the threshold.
[2]
2
=> 1

# A single even element above the threshold.
[6]
5
=> 0

# The whole array is a chain; its first element equals the threshold.
[10,3,8,1]
10
=> 4

# The parities alternate from the start, but the first element is odd.
[3,4,5,6,7]
100
=> 4

# Two evens in a row: the second one starts a new chain, the longest.
[2,4,1,2,3,6]
10
=> 5

# Two odds in a row: no chain starts at the second one.
[2,1,1,2,3,4]
10
=> 3

# An even element above the threshold splits an alternating stretch.
[6,7,8,9,60,11,12,13,14]

=> 4

# An odd element above the threshold splits it too.
[8,51,8,1]

=> 2

# An even element just above the threshold sits between an odd and an even
# one: the chains on its two sides must not be joined across it.
