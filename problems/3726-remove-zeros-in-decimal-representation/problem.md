title: Remove Zeros in Decimal Representation
difficulty: easy
tags: math, simulation
signature: long removeZeros(long n)
---
Write the positive integer `n` in decimal and erase every digit `0` from it,
wherever it is: at the end, in the middle, alone or in a run. The digits
left, kept in the order they had, form a new number. Return that number.

The first digit of `n` is never `0`, so at least one digit is always left.

@examples

## Constraints

- `1 <= n <= 10^15`
