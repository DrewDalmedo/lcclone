# Count the Digits That Divide a Number.

@example 315 is a multiple of 3, of 1 and of 5, so all three digits get a tick.
315
=> 3

@example 4422 is even, so both 2s get a tick, but 4422 % 4 is 2, so neither 4 does.
4422
=> 2

@example 239 is not a multiple of 2, of 3 or of 9.
239
=> 0

# The smallest num.
1
=> 1

# 32 is not a multiple of 3, though the 3 left after dropping the last
# digit is.
32
=> 1

# 135 is a multiple of 3, though 13 is not.
135
=> 3

# A repeated digit that divides num counts every time.
2222
=> 4

# The three 3s get ticks; the 6 does not, as 3363 is odd.
3363
=> 3

# Seven digits and no tick: odd, and its digit sum 17 is not a multiple
# of 3.
2233223
=> 0

# Both 5s get a tick, as num ends in 5; the 2 does not.
525
=> 2

# Nine digits, all ticks.
111111111
=> 9

# The largest num allowed, a multiple of 9.
999999999
=> 9

# 888 is a multiple of 8, and so is 888888888.
888888888
=> 9

# Nine digits and no tick: 999999998 is not a multiple of 9 or of 8.
999999998
=> 0

# Only 9, 3 and 1: num is odd, doesn't end in 5, and leaves 3 over when
# divided by 7.
987654321
=> 3

# 98412 is a multiple of 3 and of 4 but not of 9 or of 8: the 9 and the
# 8 get crosses, the 4, the 1 and the 2 ticks.
98412
=> 3
