# Count Prefixes of a Given String.

@example "s", "su", "sun" and "sunny" are prefixes of "sunny"; "un" is inside it but not at its start, and "sunk" differs at its last letter.
["sun","s","sunny","su","un","sunk"]
"sunny"
=> 4

@example "mo" is listed twice and counted twice, and "m" counts too; "moon" starts with "moo" but is longer, so it is not a prefix of it.
["mo","mo","moon","m"]
"moo"
=> 3

@example "am" and "m" sit at the end of "lam", not at its start, and "lamb" is longer than "lam".
["am","lamb","m"]
"lam"
=> 0

["planet","planets","plane"]
"planet"
=> 2

# Words that match s except at their last letter.
["pond","poni","pon"]
"pony"
=> 1

# Words whose first and last letters agree with s but a middle letter
# differs.
["slam","sta","spa","stamp","stump"]
"stamp"
=> 2

# Words that begin with s are not prefixes of it, unless equal to it.
["tree","trees","treetop","tr"]
"tree"
=> 2

# Pieces of s that are not at its start, one of them at its end.
["ight","night","ni","t","igh"]
"night"
=> 2
