# Rearrange Spaces Between Words.

@example 3 words and 5 spaces: 2 spaces in each of the 2 gaps, and the 1 left over goes after the last word.
"  red  fox jumps"
=> "red  fox  jumps "

@example 3 words and 8 spaces: 4 in each gap, with none left over.
" sun   moon  stars  "
=> "sun    moon    stars"

@example A single word moves to the front, and all 4 spaces follow it.
"   zebra "
=> "zebra    "

# The shortest line: a single letter.
"q"
=> "q"

# One word: spaces only before it, only after it, and on both sides.
"   lead"
=> "lead   "

"tail    "
=> "tail    "

"  both  "
=> "both    "

# One-letter words with one space between each: already laid out.
"a b c d e f g h"
=> "a b c d e f g h"

# Already laid out, with one space left over at the end (5 spaces, 3 words).
"ab  cd  ef "
=> "ab  cd  ef "

# The most spaces that can be left over: 7 spaces and 5 words give gaps of 1
# and w - 2 = 3 spaces after the last word.
"  one two three four five "
=> "one two three four five   "

# Two words: every space goes into the one gap, and none trails.
"  m      n   "
=> "m           n"

# Words move right: spaces of the second gap move into the first, so writing
# over text from the left would overwrite b before it is read.
"a b      c"
=> "a   b   c "

# The longest line with two words: 98 spaces in the one gap.
"                                                 a                                                 b"
=> "a                                                                                                  b"

# 50 one-letter words (the most 100 characters can hold) and 50 spaces: gaps
# of 1, and the space that was in front goes to the end.
" a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x"
=> "a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x "

# One word of 100 letters.
"abcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghij"
=> "abcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghijabcdefghij"
