# Minimum Cuts to Divide a Circle.

@example Three long cuts, each turned 60 degrees from the one before, give six equal wedges.
6
=> 3

@example The edges of five equal wedges are 72 degrees apart and no two lie in one line, so each edge takes a short cut.
5
=> 5

@example One slice is the uncut pizza.
1
=> 0

# Two slices: a single long cut.
2
=> 1

# Seven slices: no two edges are opposite, so no long cut fits.
7
=> 7

# Even counts take long cuts only.
