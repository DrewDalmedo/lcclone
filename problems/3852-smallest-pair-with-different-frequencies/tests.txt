# Smallest Pair With Different Frequencies. No pair is written [-1,-1].

@example 2, 5 and 8 occur twice and 11 once, so the pairs of different heights are (2, 11), (5, 11) and (8, 11).
[5,8,2,8,2,11,5]
=> [2,11]

@example 4 and 7 both occur twice: their stacks are equally tall.
[7,4,7,4]
=> [-1,-1]

@example 5 occurs once and 12 twice.
[12,5,12]
=> [5,12]

# Each number three times, with absent numbers (3, 4, 6, 7, 8) between them.
[9,2,9,5,2,5,9,5,2]
=> [-1,-1]

# 2 is absent from nums, so it can't be y even though it is just above 1.
[1,3,3]
=> [1,3]

# The smallest number stands last, copies are not adjacent, and y = x + 1.
[40,41,40,39]
=> [39,40]

# y appears less often than x.
[7,5,6,5,7,5,7]
=> [5,6]

# 2, 3, 4 and 5 have the same height as 1; 6 is the first whose height differs.
[8,1,4,6,2,6,3,7,5]

# 5 and 8 are as tall as 3, while 7 and 9 are not: y is the smaller of those, 7.
[8,3,5,8,3,5,9,7]
=> [3,7]
