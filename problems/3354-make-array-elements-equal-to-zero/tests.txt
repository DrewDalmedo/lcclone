# Make Array Elements Equal to Zero.

@example From cell 1 there are 2 hits to the left and 2 to the right. The ball wears both sides down one hit at a time, whichever way it starts, so both launches clear the row.
[2,0,1,1]
=> 2

@example Only the launch from cell 2 heading left works: it hits the 4 first, so the left side runs out last and the ball leaves on the right. Heading right first, the 3 runs out while the 4 still has a hit left. From cell 0 the ball soon leaves on the left with walls standing.
[0,4,0,3]
=> 1

@example Both empty cells have 3 hits on their left and 6 on their right, too far apart: every launch leaves walls standing.
[3,0,0,6]
=> 0
