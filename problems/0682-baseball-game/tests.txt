# Baseball Game.

@example 6, -2 and their sum 4 are printed, then 8; "C" cuts off the 8 and 9 is printed, so the tape holds 6 - 2 + 4 + 9 = 17.
["6","-2","+","D","C","9"]
=> 17

@example "C" leaves the tape blank; then -1 is printed and doubled twice: -1 - 2 - 4 = -7.
["15","C","-1","D","D"]
=> -7

@example The two "C"s cut off 4 and then -3, so "D" doubles the 7 that is at the bottom again: 7 + 14 = 21.
["7","-3","4","C","C","D"]
=> 21

=> 0

# The smallest input: a single integer, at the lower bound.
["-30000"]
=> -30000

# "0" is an integer like any other: 0 + 0 + 3 + 3.
["0","D","3","+"]
=> 6

# Negative numbers feeding "+" and "D": -4 - 6 - 10 - 20.
["-4","-6","+","D"]
=> -40

# Two "C"s in a row bring 12 and 5 back to the bottom: 12 + 5 + 17 + 34.
["12","5","-3","8","C","C","+","D"]
=> 68

# "C" cuts off the sum that "+" printed; "D" then doubles the 4: 3 + 4 + 8.
["3","4","+","C","D"]
=> 15

# "C" cuts off what "D" printed; "+" then adds 10 and -25: 10 - 25 - 15.
["10","-25","D","C","+"]
=> -30

# "D" right after "+", and "+" right after "D": 2 - 5 - 3 - 6 - 9.
["2","-5","+","D","+"]
=> -21

# The tape goes blank twice and fills again; only 26 is left at the end.
["8","C","-1","C","26","D","C"]
=> 26
