# Largest Triangle Area. compare defaults to float 1e-5 for a double; every
# exact answer is a multiple of 0.5.

@example (0,0), (4,0) and (0,3) form a right triangle with legs 4 and 3, so its area is 6. Every triangle using (1,1) is smaller.
[[0,0],[4,0],[0,3],[1,1]]
=> 6.0

@example Three points make just one triangle, and its area is 6.5.
[[-2,1],[1,-1],[3,2]]
=> 6.5

@example All the points lie on the line y = x, so every triangle is flat.
[[1,1],[2,2],[3,3],[5,5]]
=> 0.0

# Three corners of the whole range: the largest area possible.
[[-50,-50],[50,-50],[-50,50]]
=> 5000.0

# All four corners of the range: any three of them give the largest area.
[[-50,-50],[50,-50],[50,50],[-50,50]]
=> 5000.0

# Three points on a vertical line.
[[7,-50],[7,0],[7,50]]
=> 0.0

# Four points on the line y = -2x, with negative coordinates.
[[-3,6],[-1,2],[0,0],[2,-4]]
=> 0.0

# A thin triangle with the smallest area that isn't 0: the .5 must not be lost.
[[2,3],[3,5],[4,6]]
=> 0.5

# Corners listed clockwise, so the cross product is negative.
[[0,0],[0,5],[3,0]]
=> 7.5

# The point with the largest x, (21,15), is not a corner of the best triangle.
[[21,15],[0,0],[20,0],[10,30]]
=> 300.0

# Points in all four quadrants, and one inside them all.
[[-30,20],[25,40],[45,-35],[-40,-45],[0,0]]
=> 3287.5

# Five points on a line and one off it: the longest base with that apex.
