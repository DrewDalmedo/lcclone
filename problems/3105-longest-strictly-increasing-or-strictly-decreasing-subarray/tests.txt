# Longest Strictly Increasing or Strictly Decreasing Subarray.

[2,5,9,9,7,4,1,6]
=> 4

@example 8, 3 is falling and 3, 5, 10, 12 is rising; the 3 belongs to both.
[8,3,5,10,12]
=> 4

@example The values go up and down by turns, so no segment of three works.

=> 2

[1,3,5,5,7,9]
=> 3

# A flat step in the middle of a fall, and another near its end.
[9,8,8,7,6,5,5,4]
=> 4

# A peak, then the longest run: the fall starts at the 9.
[2,4,9,7,5,3,1]

[7,7,8,9]
=> 3

# The longest run ends the array, after a flat step and a valley.
[5,4,4,1,2,3,4,5,6]

[1,9,2,3,8,4,5]
