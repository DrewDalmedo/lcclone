# Alternating Groups I.

@example Bead 3 (a 0 between two 1s) and bead 4 (a 1 between two 0s) differ from both neighbours; every other bead matches one of its neighbours.
[0,1,1,0,1,0]
=> 2

@example Around the loop, bead 0 sits between bead 4 and bead 1, both 1s, so the triple centred on bead 0 counts. No other triple does.
[0,1,1,1,1]
=> 1

@example The colors alternate all the way round, so all six triples count.
[1,0,1,0,1,0]
=> 6
