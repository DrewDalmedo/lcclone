# XOR Operation in an Array.

@example The numbers are 4, 6 and 8: 4 XOR 6 is 2, and 2 XOR 8 is 10.
3
4
=> 10

@example The numbers are 1, 3, 5 and 7: 1 XOR 3 is 2, 2 XOR 5 is 7, and 7 XOR 7 is 0.
4
1
=> 0

@example 6 is 0110 in binary and 8 is 1000; they share no set bit, so the XOR is 1110, which is 14.
2
6
=> 14
