# Count Days Spent Together. The inputs are arriveAlice, leaveAlice, arriveBob
# and leaveBob, in that order.

@example Both are there from 25 February to 5 March: 4 days of February and 5 of March.
"02-20"
"03-05"
"02-25"
"04-10"
=> 9

@example 20 November is Alice's last day and Bob's first, and the only day they share.
"11-03"
"11-20"
"11-20"
"12-02"
=> 1

@example Bob's whole stay falls inside Alice's: 3 days of April and 3 of May.
"01-01"
"12-31"
"04-28"
"05-03"
