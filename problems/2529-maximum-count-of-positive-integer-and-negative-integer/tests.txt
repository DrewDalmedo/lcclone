@example Three accounts are overdrawn (-7, -4 and -4) and two are in credit (3 and 9).
[-7,-4,-4,0,3,9]
=> 3

@example The three zeros belong to neither group: one account is overdrawn and four are in credit.
[-5,0,0,0,2,6,8,8]
=> 4

@example Every balance is 0, so both counts are 0.
[0,0,0]
=> 0

[0]

[-9,-9,-3,-1]

# A tie between the two counts.
[-3,-1,2,4]

[-8,-6,-6,-2,0,1,1,3,7]

[0,0,4]
