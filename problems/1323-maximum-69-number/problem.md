title: Maximum 69 Number
difficulty: easy

---
Every digit of the positive integer `num` is a 6 or a 9. Picture the
digits printed on separate cards, laid out in the same order. You may turn
**at most one** card upside down, which makes a 6 into a 9 or a 9 into a
6. Turning none is allowed too.

Return the largest number the cards can show when you are done.

@examples

## Constraints

- `1 <= num <= 10^4`
- Every digit of `num` is `6` or `9`.
