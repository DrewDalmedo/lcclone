# Maximum 69 Number. There are only 30 valid inputs; the examples and the
# hand-written cases below cover every one of them.

@example Turning the first card gives 9696; turning any other card gives at most 6996.
6696
=> 9696

@example Turning the 6 gives 99.
96
=> 99

@example Every card already shows a 9, and turning one would only make the number smaller, so leave them all.
999
=> 999
